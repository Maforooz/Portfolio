About

I'm Mahtab, a former physics student turned interaction designer — currently studying at Emily Carr University. My journey into design wasn't a straight line. While I was deep in the world of formulas and logic, I found myself craving creativity — something that would let me build things people could feel, touch, and interact with. That curiosity led me to shift paths and dive into the world of interaction design. What's stayed the same, though, is my love for problem-solving. Whether it was exploring the mysteries of the universe through physics, or designing meaningful digital experiences, I've always been driven by the challenge of making complex things feel simple and intuitive.

Right now, I'm looking for opportunities where I can grow, experiment, and contribute to real-world projects that make a difference. I'm passionate, curious, and always ready to learn something new.
